Most projects are delivered within 48–72 hours of receiving your content and requirements. Complex builds — multi-page applications, custom animations, or headless CMS integrations — are scoped clearly upfront with a defined timeline before any work begins.

Yes — full ownership, no exceptions. You receive the complete source code, all assets, and full deployment access. No retainer, no licence fees, no platform lock-in. The site is yours permanently and can be moved or modified by any developer at any time.

The framework and language stack is always chosen to match the project's scope and requirements — there's no one-size-fits-all approach. Marketing sites and landing pages are built with semantic HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript for maximum performance and zero bloat. Dynamic web applications use React, Next.js, and TypeScript paired with Tailwind CSS for styling, and Prisma or Supabase for data. CMS-driven builds use Sanity or Contentful. Every project is production-grade, fully typed, and version-controlled from day one.
